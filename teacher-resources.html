Implementation and provenance

Teacher resources

A source-grounded view of the ten-module student pathway, curriculum connections and local decisions that remain under teacher control.

Source boundary

The course was built from the open Lunch Is Packed Classroom and Drive teaching sequence: safety, textiles, research, machine knowledge, design ideas, designer study, materials, construction, testing and evaluation.

Private student submissions, named student folders and sample photographs were excluded. Where linked or shortcut files could not be reliably read, the site preserves the topic boundary and directs students to the current teacher-approved source.

Teacher to confirm

Formal assessment status, dates, marks, submission method, exact pattern, dimensions, materials, machine model and settings, lesson allocation, equipment access, risk controls, supervision and adjustments.

The site supports learning; it does not replace current school procedures or a task notification.
